07 Spillover Tiers

Cascading Impact: Four Tiers of Vulnerability

Not all developing countries were affected equally. The tightening cycle sorted nations into four tiers of impact — from sovereign default to managed absorption.

The Debt Distress Threshold

Countries with debt service ratios above 25% of exports and USD-denominated debt exceeding 60% were most vulnerable to the tightening cycle. The combination of rising rates and dollar strength created a compounding effect that pushed 25 countries into distress.

12 Spillback Loop

The Feedback Loop: How Spillovers Spill Back

Monetary tightening doesn't flow in one direction. When developing countries destabilize, the effects reverberate back to advanced economies through trade, banking, and confidence channels.

@defer (on viewport) { } @placeholder {
Loading spillback diagram...

The Spillback Cost

The Peterson Institute estimates that US monetary tightening spillovers reduced US export demand by approximately $45 billion through 2023 as developing country growth collapsed. The global economy is a system — there is no tightening without consequence.
